# 4. CARD WIDTH PRINCIPLE

Apply the corrections independently to each plan.

Do not copy the same widths across:

- HR Basic and Payroll
- HR Standard
- HR Premium

Each plan has a different set of cards and should use its available row width differently.

Use the existing fluid card-width system.

The final card widths should be based on:

- Actual content density
- Tab requirements
- Data complexity
- Available row space
- The other cards visible in that plan

Cards should:

- Have enough width to display their information properly.
- Not have unnecessary empty width.
- Not be compressed.
- Not be stretched simply to fill a row.
- Professionally hug their content.
- Fit together cleanly within their rows.

Do not force all cards to have equal widths.

Do not force all rows to contain the same number of cards.

Do not change card widths that are not specifically listed in this prompt.
