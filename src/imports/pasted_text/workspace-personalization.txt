"Your dashboard,
your way"

"Personalise your workspace to reflect your role,

The current visual contains:
- Attendance Today
- Quick Actions

REMOVE BOTH COMPLETELY.

Do not replace them with another pair of feature-specific cards.

==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

This slide is about PERSONALISATION OF THE WORKSPACE.

It should communicate:

"Every employee can arrange their workspace around the information that matters to them."

It should NOT communicate:

"NotchHR has Attendance and Quick Actions."

Therefore, use generic workspace content that is applicable across different NotchHR plans and organizations.

==================================================
USE GENERIC WORKSPACE CARDS
==================================================

Build the visual using actual existing workspace cards/components from the product.

Preferred cards/content:

1. Announcements
2. Employee Directory
3. Document Hub / Documents

Optional:
- Events
- Employee Profile
- Leave Summary
- Company Information

Prioritize cards that are broadly useful regardless of the organization's subscription configuration.

Do NOT use:
- Attendance
- Quick Actions
- Payroll
- Salary
- Employee Benefits
- Salary on Demand
- E-Wallet
- other plan-dependent/specialized modules

==================================================
IMPORTANT — USE THE REAL COMPONENTS
==================================================

Do NOT invent simplified fake cards.

Reuse the actual card components and visual language already present in the Employee Workspace.

The visual should look like a REAL SECTION OF THE PRODUCT.

Show the cards inside an actual workspace/dashboard context.

==================================================
PERSONALISATION STATE
==================================================

The most important part of this slide is showing that the cards can be rearranged.

Show approximately 3 generic cards arranged in a smart workspace layout.

For example:

┌──────────────────────────────┐

==================================================
SHOW PERSONALISATION WITHOUT MAKING IT LOOK LIKE A
DRAG-AND-DROP TUTORIAL
==================================================

Do NOT put giant arrows everywhere.

Do NOT create empty dashed boxes.

Do NOT make the visual look like a wireframe.

Instead, show a polished real-product state with a subtle indication that the cards are movable.

For example:

- one card slightly offset
- subtle drag handle
- very subtle movement cue
- selected card state
- light elevation on the active card

Keep it elegant and premium.

==================================================
SMART GRID
==================================================

The cards must follow the Smart Fluid Grid system already being developed.

Use:

- 16px horizontal gap
- 16px vertical gap
- content-aware card widths
- cards that expand or contract according to available space
- no unnecessary empty space
- no squeezed content
- no arbitrary equal-width cards when the content doesn't require it

The composition should demonstrate that the workspace intelligently accommodates different card sizes.

==================================================
VISUAL COMPOSITION
==================================================

The visual should occupy the same approximate showcase area as the current Slide 02 visual.

Do not make the cards tiny.

Do not stretch individual cards unnaturally.

Do not create a giant empty dashboard.

Show a believable proportional crop of the Employee Workspace containing these generic cards.

The visual should feel like:

"Here is a real employee workspace that has been arranged to suit the employee."

==================================================
PLAN INDEPENDENCE
==================================================

This is critical.

The visual must remain meaningful even if:

- Attendance is not enabled
- Payroll is not enabled
- Employee Benefits is not enabled
- specialized financial modules are not enabled

Therefore, use only broadly applicable workspace content.

==================================================
FINAL VISUAL MESSAGE
==================================================

The user should look at the slide and immediately understand:

"I can make my workspace work for me."

Not:

"I can check my attendance."

Not:

"I can access quick actions."

The visual is demonstrating WORKSPACE PERSONALISATION.

Keep the existing NotchHR visual language, typography, purple/magenta brand treatment, spacing and premium SaaS aesthetic.
